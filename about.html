About UK Plug

UK-managed. Bangladesh-made. Internationally supplied.

Your international packaging sourcing partner.

Connecting global businesses with qualified manufacturing partners and competitive production capabilities. Paper-based packaging for FMCG, food and beverage, cosmetics and personal care, tobacco, electronics, footwear, jewellery and ready-made garments.

Who we are.

UK Plug Sourcing Limited is a United Kingdom-based international packaging sourcing and supply-chain company. We specialise in sourcing, developing and coordinating paper-based packaging solutions for international customers across a diverse range of industries.

Our business combines UK-based international commercial management with access to established manufacturing networks in Bangladesh and other sourcing markets. Through carefully selected production partners, we provide commercially competitive packaging while maintaining close attention to product specifications, manufacturing standards, quality requirements and delivery commitments.

Our services cover the complete sourcing process, from manufacturer identification, technical development and commercial negotiation to production management, quality assurance, export documentation and shipment coordination. We aim to give international customers a single, coordinated sourcing relationship, simplifying the complexities of overseas manufacturing and procurement.

Why Bangladesh

A strategic manufacturing and sourcing destination.

Bangladesh is an important manufacturing and sourcing destination within our international supply network. It has developed a substantial export-oriented industrial base, supported by an experienced production workforce, competitive labour costs and an extensive network of manufacturers and supporting industries.

The established ready-made garments sector has contributed to a broad industrial ecosystem, including printing, packaging, labels, accessories, paper products and related manufacturing services. For international buyers, that means competitive production costs, experienced personnel and a wide supplier network.

Our objective is not simply to find the lowest-cost manufacturer. We focus on identifying the right manufacturing partner for each customer's product, quality expectations, compliance requirements and commercial objectives.
