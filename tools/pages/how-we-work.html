How we work

Six stages. One accountable partner.

Every stage ends with something you sign off.

Procurement needs to know who owns each decision and what evidence sits behind it. This is the sequence, the sign-off at each stage, and the checks that run through it.

The six stages

01

Brief

You send the product, quantity, specification, market and date, or as much of it as you know. We reply with the questions a quotation needs and confirm the brief in writing.

Ends withA confirmed brief
02

Source

The product is matched to the manufacturing partner with the right press, finishing, construction and capacity, and quoted on landed value: factory, materials, packing, freight, lead time and risk.

Ends withA route and a quotation
03

Develop

Structure, dieline, material and finish are developed and sampled before anything is ordered. The approved sample becomes the standard the run is checked against.

Ends withAn approved sample
04

Approve

Colour references, artwork proofs and the sample are signed off, and the quality plan is agreed with you and the factory: what is checked, when, by whom, and against what.

Ends withA signed specification
05

Produce

Printing, finishing and converting run to the plan, with progress reported and checks carried out in line by the Averon team on site at the partner factory.

Ends withInspected stock
06

Deliver

Packing, export documentation and freight are coordinated to the destination, and the file stays open until stock is received and confirmed.

Ends withStock received
Quality management

Landed value

Beyond factory price.

A factory quote is one number out of six. Averon quotes the landed value, and every term in it is visible before you commit.

01Factory+

The unit price at the factory gate. The number most comparisons stop at.

02Materials+

Board grade, grammage and finish decide cost, weight and how the pack performs.

03Packing+

Export cartons, pallets and protection decide how much arrives undamaged.

04Freight+

Mode, route and container utilisation, and the documentation between them.

05Lead time+

The weeks from order to delivery, and what a missed launch date costs.

06Risk=

Who owns a problem at each stage, and whether anyone checks before it ships.
