Privacy

How UK Plug Sourcing Limited handles the information you send us.

This page explains what information UK Plug Sourcing Limited ("UK Plug", "we") receives through this website and how it is used.

What this site collects

The site itself stores nothing about you. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics or advertising scripts, and loads its fonts, scripts and images from its own domain.

Starting a project

The Start a Project form prepares an email in your own mail application. Nothing is sent from this website; the brief leaves your device only when you send that email. The information you include, such as your name, company, email address, telephone number and the details of your packaging requirement, is used to reply to you, prepare a quotation and, if you proceed, manage your project. It is shared with a manufacturing partner only to the extent needed to quote or produce your packaging.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what information we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or ask us to stop contacting you. Write to us at the address on your correspondence with UK Plug.

Changes

This page is updated when our practices change. It was last reviewed in October 2026.
