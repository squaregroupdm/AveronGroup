# Surface: index.html (Averon Global homepage, rebuild 2)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: a procurement or brand manager (UK/EU brand, or an international buyer of a Bangladeshi exporter) deciding in under a minute whether to start a project with Averon. Job: understand what Averon does, believe it understands manufacturing and supply, start a project (brief.html). Proof on hand: the company profile and 32 generated factory, product and QC photographs. No clients, awards, certifications, prices or partner names are published; placeholders are labelled. Constraints: the owner's rebuild brief of October 2026 pins the grid (12 columns, 1280px max, 48 to 72 / 32 / 20 margins), the type scale (H1 56 to 68, section 38 to 48, body 16 to 17, labels 11 to 12, nav and buttons 13 to 14), the spacing scale (8 to 112, sections 88 to 112), the section order, the colour rhythm, 40 to 50% photography, rows over cards, 4 to 8px radii, 42 to 46px buttons, and bans parallax, pinned sequences, continuous motion and full-viewport sections. The brief also asks for small eyebrows on the hero and quality sections and 01 to 06 numbering on the capability and stage lists; those user instructions stand over the craft floor. No em dashes.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage is the technical prospectus of an international supply house: one disciplined grid, thin rules, numbered rows, contained photographs, information readable several items per screen. It refuses the cinematic portfolio (full-bleed hero, pinned stages, giant display type) and the SaaS template (rounded cards with icons).

OWN-WORLD: Ivory, white and midnight navy alternate as section grounds in the owner's rhythm; ink navy for the footer; slate for secondary text on light grounds, ivory at 72% on navy. STIX Two Text carries headings and body; Archivo carries labels, navigation, numbers and buttons at small sizes, uppercase and tracked for labels. Rules are 1px navy at 14% on light and ivory at 18% on dark. Radii are 4px on buttons and images; nothing else is rounded. No shadows, no gradients. Photographs sit inside grid columns at 4:3, 3:2, 16:10 or 3:4, never full-bleed.

STORY: The visitor reads what Averon does in the hero, scans the four disciplines in one screen, browses six capabilities, sees the six-stage process in one contained component, recognises their industry, reads the quality checklist like a report, sees the UK to Bangladesh route, understands landed value, and starts a project.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 68px navigation with a small mark and wordmark, five links, bordered "Start a Project" button. Below it a split hero at 74vh maximum: left five columns on ivory with eyebrow INTERNATIONAL SOURCING & SUPPLY, two-line H1 "From requirement to delivery." at 64px, 18px support copy, primary and secondary buttons, then the line "United Kingdom / Bangladesh / International Supply"; right seven columns a contained press photograph, 4px radius, no text over it. Under the hero a thin four-item positioning row.

FORM: brief-pinned direction. The owner's rebuild brief of October 2026 pins grid, type, spacing, section order, colour rhythm and interaction, so concept-seed was not run under new-work section 3 (a precisely specified request is shaped directly; a brief-pinned direction beats the roll). seed: none (unavailable).

Signature interaction: the six-stage process component, where selecting a stage moves a thin progress line and crossfades one photograph and one paragraph. Motion grammar: 240ms crossfades, 4px arrow travel, 1.02 image zoom on hover, navigation underline growing from the left, 600ms opacity and 12px rise on scroll reveal, the route line drawing once. Nothing loops.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
