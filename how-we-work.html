How we work

Eight steps. One coordinated relationship.

Simple for the customer. Detailed behind the scenes.

UK Plug Sourcing Limited provides a coordinated sourcing and commercial relationship. Depending on the transaction structure, your requirements and the applicable legal and regulatory arrangements, the model runs in this sequence.

The commercial model

01

Requirement

You communicate product requirements, specifications, quantities and delivery expectations to UK Plug Sourcing Limited.

Ends withA confirmed brief
02

Sourcing

We identify suitable manufacturing partners and obtain competitive quotations, evaluated against the specification, quality expectation and commercial requirement.

Ends withA quotation
03

Technical development

We coordinate technical discussions, samples and product approvals: structure, material, GSM, artwork, colour, die-line and finishing feasibility, then prototypes and pre-production samples.

Ends withAn approved sample
04

Commercial terms

Commercial terms, order responsibilities and payment arrangements are agreed between the relevant parties.

Ends withAgreed terms
05

Manufacturing

Production is coordinated through the selected manufacturing partner, with local oversight from our Bangladesh operation: planning, raw-material coordination, progress monitoring and follow-up.

Ends withProduction under way
06

Quality

Quality inspections and production monitoring are conducted according to agreed requirements, from approved-sample verification to pre-shipment inspection.

Ends withInspected stock
07

Export

Export documentation, freight arrangements and shipment coordination are completed through the relevant authorised parties.

Ends withA booked shipment
08

Delivery

Delivery and customer payment are handled according to the agreed contractual and trade arrangements.

Ends withStock received
Two entities, one structure

UK Plug Sourcing Limited in the United Kingdom manages the customer relationship, quotations, commercial negotiation and order management. UK Plug Sourcing Bangladesh Limited manages local sourcing, factory coordination, sampling, production monitoring, inspection and export execution.

Commercial structures

Where appropriate, UK Plug may explore arrangements involving direct customer supply, international trading, approved trade finance or other documented commercial structures.

Subject to

All transactions remain subject to applicable UK and Bangladesh laws, banking requirements, foreign-exchange regulations, customs rules, taxation, trade-finance conditions and contractual obligations.

Quality management

Visibility and control at every stage.

Our quality-management approach is designed to give you greater visibility and control throughout manufacturing. Checks are coordinated at six stages, and inspection methods and acceptance criteria are agreed according to the product, your requirements and the relevant standards. The objective is to identify potential production issues early, support corrective action and improve consistency across orders.

Six stages
  • Supplier assessmentCapability, experience, equipment and quality systems
  • Technical approvalSpecifications, materials, dimensions, artwork, colours, finishing
  • Sample approvalPrototypes and pre-production samples for your review
  • Production monitoringProgress and compliance with the approved specification
  • Quality inspectionAgreed acceptance criteria and sampling requirements
  • Pre-shipment verificationFinished goods, packing and shipment readiness before dispatch
What is checked
  • MaterialsApproved sample verification, raw material and paperboard checks
  • PrintPrinting quality, colour consistency, registration
  • FinishingLamination and coating, finishing and assembly
  • ConstructionDie-cutting accuracy, folding and gluing, dimensions, carton strength where applicable
  • ShipmentPre-shipment inspection, non-conformity reporting and corrective action follow-up
Supplier qualification & compliance

Capable, compliant and responsible partners.

We aim to work with manufacturing partners that demonstrate suitable production capability, quality-management systems, environmental responsibility and relevant social or food-safety compliance. Depending on product category and customer requirements, supplier assessments may include the following.

ISO 9001

Quality management systems. Assessment of a manufacturer's quality-management processes and systems.

ISO 14001

Environmental management systems. Environmental management practices and related operational controls.

GMI

Graphic Measures International. Print quality assessment and consistency in packaging production.

FSC

Forest Stewardship Council. Responsible sourcing of forest-based materials and certified paper supply chains. Chain of Custody certification and claims must be verified.

FSSC 22000

Food safety management. Manufacturing operations within the applicable food-safety certification scope.

SQF

Safe Quality Food. A food-safety and quality programme relevant to qualifying food-related operations.

SEDEX

Supplier Ethical Data Exchange. Supply-chain transparency and ethical business information sharing.

SMETA

Sedex Members Ethical Trade Audit. Labour standards, health and safety, environment and business ethics.

Certification and audit requirements are assessed according to the customer's product, market, industry and compliance expectations. We verify the relevant factory, certification scope, validity and applicability rather than assuming that every supplier holds every listed credential.
