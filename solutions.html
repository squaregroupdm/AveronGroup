Solutions

Six core services. One coordinated relationship.

From manufacturer to shipment, coordinated.

Our services cover the complete sourcing process: manufacturer identification, technical development, commercial negotiation, production management, quality assurance, export documentation and shipment coordination, through a single point of contact.

Core services

01

Strategic Sourcing & Procurement

We identify and evaluate suitable manufacturing partners according to product specifications, production capabilities, quality expectations and commercial requirements. The objective is not simply the lowest-cost manufacturer but the right partner for each product, quality expectation, compliance requirement and commercial objective.

  • Manufacturer identification and screening
  • Factory capability assessments
  • Quotation collection
  • Cost comparison
  • Feasibility assessment
  • Supplier negotiation
  • Partner selection
  • Relationship management
02

Technical Development & Product Engineering

We coordinate the technical development process so that packaging products meet the customer requirement before mass production: structure, paperboard and material selection, GSM and thickness, dimensions and construction, artwork and prepress, colour separation, Pantone and process colour management, die-line development, finishing feasibility and pre-production sampling.

  • Structure development
  • Material selection
  • GSM and thickness
  • Dimensions and construction
  • Artwork and prepress
  • Colour management
  • Die-line development
  • Prototype and sampling
03

Production Management

Local production coordination from our Bangladesh entity helps ensure orders progress according to agreed specifications and schedules: planning and scheduling, supplier communication, raw-material coordination, progress monitoring, manufacturing follow-up, issue identification, delivery schedule monitoring and order status reporting.

  • Production planning
  • Supplier communication
  • Raw-material coordination
  • Progress monitoring
  • Issue identification
  • Order status reporting
04

Quality Assurance & Inspection

We coordinate inspections and production checks according to agreed customer specifications and acceptance criteria: approved sample verification, raw material and paperboard checks, printing quality, colour consistency and registration, lamination and coating, die-cutting accuracy, folding and gluing, finishing and assembly, dimensions, carton strength where applicable, pre-shipment inspection and non-conformity reporting with corrective action follow-up.

  • Approved sample verification
  • Material and paperboard checks
  • Print and colour
  • Die-cut, fold and glue
  • Dimensional verification
  • Pre-shipment inspection
  • Non-conformity reporting
05

Export & Logistics Coordination

We coordinate export preparation and the shipment process with manufacturers, freight forwarders and other relevant service providers: export documentation, commercial invoice and packing list preparation support, shipment readiness monitoring, freight-forwarder communication, cargo consolidation, container loading, shipment tracking and delivery planning.

  • Export documentation
  • Invoice and packing list support
  • Shipment readiness
  • Freight-forwarder communication
  • Consolidation
  • Container loading
  • Shipment tracking
  • Delivery planning
06

Supply-Chain Management

A coordinated point of communication throughout the order lifecycle: supplier performance monitoring, order tracking and reporting, production schedule coordination, commercial communication, delivery follow-up, continuous supplier evaluation and long-term sourcing relationship development.

  • Supplier performance monitoring
  • Order tracking and reporting
  • Schedule coordination
  • Commercial communication
  • Delivery follow-up
  • Continuous supplier evaluation

Manufacturing capabilities

Verified at supplier level, before the order.

We work with nominated manufacturing partners that have the machinery, technical personnel and production capability for the product category required. Nothing is allocated on the basis of a brochure.

Preferred press manufacturers

Heidelberg, Komori, KBA and Manroland sheetfed offset presses, verified at the partner before allocation.

Production capabilities

2 to 6 colour printing, UV and aqueous coating, drip-off coating, metallic and special colour printing, accurate registration control, consistent reproduction.

Converting

Heidelberg cylinder and Bobst die cutters, Bobst-standard folder gluers; straight-line, crash-lock and multi-point gluing.

Embellishment

Hot and cold foil, metallic embellishment, embossing, debossing, spot UV and special texture finishes combined with print.
