Capabilities

01

Global Sourcing

We identify suitable manufacturing routes according to product specification, quantity, target cost, quality requirements, material requirements, lead time, certification and market requirements, from a nominated network of trusted manufacturing partners in Bangladesh.

  • Quantity
  • Target cost

  • Lead time

02

Development

We support the transition from an initial idea or an existing specification to a production-ready packaging solution: brief, structure, material, graphics, prototype, testing, cost engineering and approval.

  • Structure
  • Material
  • Graphics
  • Prototype
  • Testing
  • Cost engineering
  • Approval
03

Manufacturing Management

We coordinate production with selected manufacturing partners and monitor requirements throughout the production process. International clients work with Averon rather than coordinating directly with multiple factories and production teams.

  • Production scheduling
  • Progress reporting
  • On-site coordination
  • One communication channel
04

Quality Assurance

We establish and monitor product specifications, production standards and inspection requirements: before production, during production and at final inspection. Where required, inspection is aligned with AQL standards and client-specific quality procedures.

  • Pre-production approvals
  • In-production checks
  • Final inspection
  • AQL where required
05

Compliance

We identify manufacturing routes according to applicable customer, material and market requirements. Certifications are held by individual manufacturing partners and applied according to their specific scope, validity and production category, so a project is matched with the appropriate certified capability rather than a generic claim.

  • ISO
  • FSC
  • OEKO-TEX
  • GRS
  • RoHS
  • TUV-related
  • Customer-specific requirements
06

Freight & Logistics

We manage the movement of finished packaging from the manufacturing source to the destination: freight quotation, sea and air freight, consolidation, container planning, shipment scheduling, packing coordination, export documentation, freight booking and shipment tracking.

  • Freight quotation
  • Sea and air freight
  • Consolidation
  • Container planning
  • Export documentation
  • Shipment tracking
  • Destination delivery

Material selection

Product protection, printability, structural strength, appearance, finishing compatibility, cost, sustainability and supply availability. The complete requirement, not the lowest-cost substrate.

Coating

Aqueous gloss and matt, UV coating, spot UV, protective coatings.

Special effects

Hot foil, cold foil, embossing, debossing, textured effects.

Converting

Die-cutting, creasing, folding, gluing, window patching, assembly.

Sustainability

Material efficiency, recycled and certified board options, structures that use less, better carton, pallet and container utilisation, fibre-based recyclable alternatives where appropriate.

Printing and print technology

Precision printing for demanding packaging.

Colour, registration, detail, consistency and finishing all contribute to the final product. Averon works through a manufacturing network with access to established sheetfed offset press technology, and selects the production route per project rather than assigning every job to the same press.

Press platforms in the network

Heidelberg, MAN Roland, Komori Lithrone and KBA Rapida sheetfed offset presses, as published by the partner factories.

Capabilities

CMYK, Pantone and spot colours, colour matching, CTP workflow, aqueous and UV coating, matt and gloss finishes, special effects, fine-detail reproduction.

Route selection

Specification, volume, substrate, colour requirements, finishing, lead time and target cost decide which press and which partner run the job.

Premium effects

Printing and finishing are combined so the packaging communicates quality before the product is opened.
