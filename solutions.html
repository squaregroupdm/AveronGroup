Solutions

Six disciplines. One connected team.

Between requirement and delivery.

Each discipline is run by the same point of contact, so nothing is handed off and nothing is lost between the brief, the factory and the destination.

Capabilities

01

Global Sourcing

Factory selection matched to the product, the volume and the finish, from a nominated network of trusted manufacturing partners in Bangladesh. Quoted on landed value, not factory price.

  • Capability matching
  • Costing and quotation
  • Sample coordination
02

Development

Structure, dielines, material selection and pre-production samples, agreed with you before an order is placed. The sample you approve is the standard the run is checked against.

  • Dielines and structure
  • Material selection
  • Pre-production samples
03

Manufacturing Management

Production scheduling, progress reporting and on-site coordination across printing, finishing and converting, managed by the Averon team at the partner factory.

  • Production scheduling
  • Progress reporting
  • On-site coordination
04

Quality Assurance

Checks agreed before production and carried out pre-production, in line and at final inspection, with AQL sampling where agreed. Material, artwork, colour, construction and packing are each signed off.

  • Pre-production checks
  • In-line inspection
  • Final inspection and AQL
05

Compliance

Coordination of the documentation, testing and standards a destination market requires, verified per project with the partner factory. Food-contact and healthcare grades are confirmed before quotation.

  • Material declarations
  • Testing coordination
  • Factory documentation
06

Freight & Logistics

Freight booking, export documentation and delivery to the destination, with one point of contact until stock is received. Sea freight as standard; air where the date demands it.

  • Freight booking
  • Export documentation
  • Destination delivery
