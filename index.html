06Pack

Built to arrive as specified.

One carton becomes a shipment.

Finished units are packed flat or assembled, counted into master cartons built to bursting-strength, edge-crush and stacking requirements, then palletised and wrapped to the export specification. What leaves the factory is what arrives.

Master cartons in A or B flute, corrugated or virgin liner, to custom dimensions and your transport specification.
