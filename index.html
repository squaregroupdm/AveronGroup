Landed cost, not factory price

A lower factory price does not mean a lower total cost. We quote on the whole chain, from sheet to your door, and these eight things decide it.

  • Material usage
  • Printing and finishing
  • Carton and pallet fit
  • Container utilisation

A cheap sheet that ships half-empty containers is not cheap.
