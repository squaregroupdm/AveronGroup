One journey. Managed end to end.

Six stages, each with an owner at Averon and a checkpoint before the next. Nothing scales before the specification, the sample and the terms are approved.

Brief

Brief

You define the product, market, quantity, target cost and date. We turn it into a specification a factory can quote.

Source

We select the manufacturing route: the factory whose machinery, capacity and certificate scope fit the job.

Develop

Structure, material and print are resolved. Samples are made and approved before anything scales.

Produce

Production runs against the approved specification, with checkpoints agreed in advance.

Verify

Colour, construction, dimensions, finish and packing are inspected before shipment readiness is confirmed.

Deliver

Freight, export documents and delivery are coordinated to your destination, with status along the way.

    Less material, better used.

    Sustainability on a packaging programme is a set of decisions, not a claim. Where the product and the destination market allow, Averon reviews:

    • material efficiency in the structure and the layout
    • recyclable, fibre-based alternatives
    • certified materials, where suitable and supported by valid supplier evidence
    • structural optimisation of the pack and the case
    • carton, pallet and container utilisation

    Environmental claims are supported by material data or supplier documentation, or they are not made.
